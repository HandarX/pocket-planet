Pocket Planet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Pocket Planet is made by Handar ("we"). It turns your daily habits into a small 3D planet that grows. This policy explains what the app uses, what stays on your phone, and what is shared.

Health data (Health Connect)

With your permission, the app reads steps, sleep, exercise sessions and water intake from Health Connect on Android. These readings are used only on your phone to decide how your planet grows each day. Your health numbers are never uploaded, sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Cloud backup

To keep your planet safe, the app backs up your planet's progress (for example its name, level, trees grown, items found and "what grew" each day) to our backup service (Supabase), using an anonymous ID created on your phone. Your own name, email address and health numbers are not part of this backup.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an ad when the app opens, and optional ads you choose to watch for a reward. We request non-personalised ads only. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. In the European Union and the UK, you are asked for consent first and can decline. Planet+ members don't see ads.

Purchases

Planet+ and Items+ are bought through Google Play. Purchases are handled by Google Play and our purchase service (RevenueCat), which receive an anonymous app user ID and your purchase details so your purchases work and can be restored. We never see your payment card details.

Children

Pocket Planet is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. To delete your cloud backup, email us and we will delete it.
